2D Apical Meristem & Zoetrope Studio
====================================

New primordia emerge at the apical meristem center at the divergence angle $\alpha$ while existing primordia accelerate radially outward and expand in size as plastochron age $k_{\text{eff}} = (N - 1) - i$ increases:

.. math::

   r_i = c\sqrt{k_{\text{eff}}}\left(\frac{k_{\text{eff}} + 1}{K_0}\right)^{\!a}, \qquad s_i = s_0 + g\sqrt{k_{\text{eff}}}\left(\frac{k_{\text{eff}} + 1}{K_0}\right)^{\!1.15a}, \qquad \theta_i = i \cdot \alpha + \phi_{\text{zoetrope}}

Use the preset bar or the collapsible control drawer (<kbd>T</kbd> to toggle, <kbd>F</kbd> for full-screen Theater Mode) to switch between **Meristem Life Cycle (Leaves → Petals → Florets → Buds)**, **Full Botanical Leaves**, **Radiant Flower Petals**, **Explorer Points** (crisp white dots with background-matching halo strokes), **Primordia Dots**, **Concentric Rings**, and **Botanical Wedges / Scales**, tune **Radial Acceleration ($a$)**, **Node Growth Factor ($g$)**, and **Life Cycle Organ Span ($F_n$)**, connect each node to its $k$ **Closest Inner Nodes** (<kbd>N</kbd>) to reveal **Curve Intersections** (<kbd>I</kbd>), or engage **Zoetrope Strobe** (<kbd>Z</kbd>). Drag to pan, scroll to zoom around the cursor, and press <kbd>0</kbd> (or double-click) to recenter.
