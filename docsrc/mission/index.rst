mission
=======

``geometor.phyllotaxis`` explores constructive and dynamic models of botanical growth grounded in the divine proportion $\phi = \frac{1 + \sqrt{5}}{2}$.

Core Principles
---------------

1. **Exact Symbolic & Rational Foundations**: Bridging exact algebraic representations of the golden angle $2\pi(1 - 1/\phi)$ and Fibonacci rational convergents with real-time visual analysis.
2. **Interactive Web Laboratories**: Replacing static batch-rendered PNG sequences with live 2D HTML5 Canvas and 3D WebGL laboratories embedded directly inside the documentation.
3. **3D Nodes as Paths & Recursive Meristems**: Modeling 3D botanical architecture where each primordium traces a developmental path $\gamma_i(\tau)$ from its origin on the parent meristem, and any node tip can mature into a child meristem.
