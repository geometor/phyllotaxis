Phyllotaxis¶

Symbolic modeling, interactive 2D/3D web visualizations, and recursive meristem path propagations of botanical geometry.

\[\Phi = 2\pi\left(1 - \frac{1}{\phi}\right) = \frac{2\pi}{\phi^2} \approx 137.507764^\circ\]

Interactive Studios¶

  • 2D Apical Meristem & Zoetrope Studio — 2D Apical Meristem, Parastichy & Zoetrope Studio: Live planar growth (\(r = c\sqrt{k}\)), rational Fibonacci convergent scrubbing (\(F_n/F_{n+2}\)), \((8, 13)\) parastichy spiral overlays, botanical wedges, and real-time \(137.5^\circ\) stroboscopic zoetrope rotation.

  • 3D Node-as-Path & Recursive Meristems — 3D Node-as-Path & Recursive Meristem Studio: Every node originates on its parent meristem’s axis and propagates outward along a 3D trajectory \(\gamma_i(\tau)\), with any node capable of maturing into a secondary or tertiary child meristem.
